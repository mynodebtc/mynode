Why Your Browser Shows a Warning

The first time you connect over HTTPS, your browser will say the connection is not private. Chrome shows "Your connection is not private", Firefox shows "Warning: Potential Security Risk Ahead" and Safari shows "This Connection Is Not Private".

This is expected and it does not mean something is broken. HTTPS certificates are normally issued by a certificate authority that verifies you own a public domain name. Your MyNode runs on your own network and has no public domain name, so it generates its own certificate instead. Your browser has no way to check who created that certificate, so it warns you rather than showing a padlock.

The connection is still encrypted. The warning is about identity, not about encryption.

Continuing to Your Node

You only need to accept the certificate once per browser and device.

Chrome, Edge and Brave — click "Advanced", then "Proceed to ... (unsafe)".

Firefox — click "Advanced", then "Accept the Risk and Continue".

Safari — click "Show Details", then "visit this website", and confirm.

iOS and Android — tap the advanced or details option on the warning page, then continue to the site.

If your browser refuses to continue at all, it is usually because the address does not match the certificate. Try reaching your node at https://myNode.local, or regenerate your certificates on the settings page if your node's IP address has changed.

Removing the Warning

To stop the warning entirely, install your node's certificate on the devices you use. Download it from the settings page under HTTPS Certificates, then mark it as trusted using your operating system's certificate or keychain settings. After that your browser treats your node like any other HTTPS site.

Your certificate is unique to your device and is generated on your node. Only install certificates you downloaded from your own MyNode.

Making HTTPS Permanent

Once HTTPS is working, turn on Force HTTPS so your node always redirects HTTP requests to HTTPS and your password is never sent unencrypted.
