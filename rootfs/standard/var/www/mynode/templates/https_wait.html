Troubleshooting steps
These steps turn off Force HTTPS. Once the device is working again, it can be turned back on in Settings.
  1. SSH to the device ssh admin@{{ ssh_host }}
  2. Remove the Force HTTPS setting from the SD card sudo rm -f /home/bitcoin/.mynode/https_forced
  3. Remove the Force HTTPS setting from the drive sudo rm -f /mnt/hdd/mynode/settings/https_forced
  4. Reboot the device sudo reboot
